The problem we solve

Construction work stalls on unclear records, unproven milestones, and disputes without evidence. GCSC keeps a clean, reviewable record so homeowners and contractors can trust the workflow — not a handshake.

Verified records

Project requests, bids, contracts, and milestone evidence live in one auditable trail with request IDs.

Evidence-first disputes

Photos, notes, peer review, and timelines are preserved so disagreements resolve on facts, not memory.

Readiness, not risk

Working-capital readiness packets are prepared for future licensed providers — no live finance today.

How a project flows

Every step leaves a verifiable record. Nothing moves on trust alone.

1

Homeowner posts a project

Scope, budget range, and timeline become a structured project record from the first minute.

2

Verified contractors bid

Profiles carry verification readiness: license and insurance status, completion history, dispute record.

3

Milestones with evidence

Work is split into milestones; each submission attaches photo and document evidence for approval.

4

Disputes resolve on facts

If something goes wrong, the dispute packet already exists — evidence, timeline, peer review.

5

Reputation compounds

Completed work becomes structured readiness data a future reviewed provider can evaluate — never a promise of value.
