Understanding DPoS

How the Delegated Proof of Stake consensus mechanism works on XPR Network.

How DPoS Works

🗳️ Voting

Each staked XPR token equals one vote. You vote directly for your preferred Block Producers.

👑 Top 21

The top 21 BPs by vote count become active validators. They produce blocks and earn daily rewards.

💎 Rewards

BPs earn rewards from 1% of XPR annual inflation. GCSC reinvests 100% into platform development.

⚡ Decentralization

Power is distributed across 21 independent producers. No single entity controls the network.
