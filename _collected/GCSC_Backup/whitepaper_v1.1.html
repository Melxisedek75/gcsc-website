8.3 What is RWA?

RWA (Real World Asset) is a digital token that represents ownership of a real-world asset: real estate, stocks, gold, rental income, revenue streams. The token does not replace the legal right — it is its digital representation, backed by a legal structure (SPV, Trust, NFT document).

Analogy: Your home is a gold bar. RWA is a certificate for that bar. You don't move the gold, but the certificate can be pledged, sold as fractions, or used as collateral — all on-chain, in seconds.

8.4 Legal Structure — SPV + Wyoming DAO LLC

⚖️ Important: In the US, real estate tokenization requires compliance with federal securities laws (SEC), state laws, and AML/KYC rules. GCSC is not a broker or lender — the platform provides infrastructure for smart contract transactions.
1

Home Valuation

Licensed appraiser determines market value. REA AI agent verifies data via Zillow/Redfin API + public registries. Result stored in NFT Property Passport on XPR Network.

2

SPV Creation — Wyoming DAO LLC

Homeowner registers an LLC in Wyoming (most crypto-friendly legislation). Home is transferred to this LLC. HET token represents ownership stake in the LLC — not the home directly. You tokenize the LLC, not the house.

3

Home Equity Token (HET) Mint

HET minted on XPR Network: $1 HET = $1 equity value. Max LTV 70%. Home worth $400K, mortgage $250K = equity $150K → up to $105,000 in HET tokens.

4

Collateral in Proton LOAN

HET deposited as collateral in Proton LOAN Protocol. Protocol issues USDC loan with 130–150% overcollateralization ratio. Rate 4–8% APR (vs 7–9% banks). Zero gas fees on XPR Network.

5

Milestone Escrow — Pay Contractor

USDC doesn't go to contractor immediately. GCSC smart contract splits payments: 20% upfront, 30% after phase 1, 30% after phase 2, 20% after final inspection. RAA AI agent monitors progress.

6

Value Growth + Refinancing

After renovation, home is worth more (US avg: 1.2x–2.0x ROI on renovations). New appraisal recalculates equity. Options: (a) repay LOAN from new equity, (b) refinance at higher valuation, (c) hold HET in liquidity pool for passive income.
