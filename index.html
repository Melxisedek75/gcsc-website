The Problem / 01

A $12T industry, still paid on handshakes and hope

Construction moves more money than almost any industry on earth — and settles it with paper invoices, fronted cash, and payment terms measured in months.

$12T

Broken at scale

Global construction runs on fragmented payments between homeowners, general contractors, subs and suppliers — with no shared rail and no shared source of truth.

90-day

Payment delays

Contractors routinely wait up to 90 days to get paid for finished work, fronting labor and materials out of pocket while invoices crawl through approval chains.

Trust with no collateral

Homeowners pay strangers up front. Contractors stake their own cash. When a job goes sideways, neither side has protection — only paperwork and a dispute.

The Solution / 02

Reputation as Collateral

A contractor's track record — not a cash bond — secures the job.

On GCSC, verified reputation is locked into the smart contract before work begins. Funds sit in programmable escrow and release only when milestones are confirmed. Do great work and your reputation compounds; fail, and the collateral you lose is the reputation you built.

Reputation locked in the contract · Escrow released per milestone

Earned, not bought

Reputation is built job by job — completed milestones, verified payments, homeowner confirmations. It cannot be purchased or faked into existence.

Locked as security

When a contractor takes a job, reputation is staked against performance — replacing the cash collateral and bonding that lock small builders out of bigger work.

Programmable escrow

The homeowner's money is committed up front and held in escrow. Each verified milestone releases its payment automatically — no invoice chasing, no 90-day wait.

GCSC Secure Payments / 04

Card and bank rails, under one white label

Escrow needs real money movement. GCSC Secure Payments pairs a white-label card gateway with account-to-account payouts — presented end to end as GCSC, never a third-party checkout.

$700B

NMI — White-Label Gateway

Card payments run on NMI's gateway — roughly $700B in annual transaction volume — fully white-labeled as GCSC Secure Payments: our domain, our branding, our merchant experience.

A2A

Dwolla — Account-to-Account

Milestone payouts move bank-to-bank through Dwolla's A2A rails, with FedNow instant payout capability — the contractor is paid when the milestone clears, not a quarter later.
