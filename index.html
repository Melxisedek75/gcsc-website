The Problem We Solve

Construction trust is fragmented across deposits, messages, bids, invoices, photos, disputes, and incomplete records. GCSC starts by making the project record clear and verifiable.

How a Project Flows Through GCSC

01 Homeowner project request

Scope, location, budget range, project notes, and request trace.

02 Contractor profile & verification readiness

Business profile, license, insurance, and identity readiness metadata.

03 Bid records & project contract

Comparable bids, scope terms, project amount, milestones, and parties.

04 Milestone evidence & approval

Photos, notes, work status, completion signals, and review context.

05 Dispute evidence & peer review

Issue packet, evidence notes, peer scoring, and review handoff.

06 Contractor reputation & history

Ratings, response behavior, completed work, and reliability signals.

07 Working-capital readiness packet

Project context and repayment-waterfall draft for a future licensed provider.

08 Admin audit trail & request IDs

Request trace, review notes, gates, and evidence routing.

09 Future reviewed infrastructure

Optional provider-reviewed records stay gated until approved.
