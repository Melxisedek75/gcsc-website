## Safe Copy Blocks

### Homepage Short Positioning

GCSC is building construction trust infrastructure through SmartContractor: a platform for project contracts, milestone evidence, contractor reputation, dispute workflows, and future compliant settlement readiness.

### Product Explanation

SmartContractor is designed to help construction participants organize bids, accepted project contracts, milestone evidence, review signals, and audit trails before money movement or public finance features are activated.

### Contractor Credit Roadmap

Contract-backed working-capital workflows are a proposed future readiness layer. A signed project contract may help organize underwriting evidence, milestone status, and repayment-first waterfall records, but it does not create a guaranteed loan, automatic approval, legal collateral, or live lender obligation.

### AI Boundary

AI-assisted workflows may help review project data, organize evidence, flag missing information, and support risk review. AI is not a final legal, lending, escrow, payment-release, or dispute authority.

### Token And Settlement Boundary

GCSC and GCST are planned utility and settlement components for the broader roadmap. Any stablecoin settlement, tokenized construction agreement, token collateral, or repayment routing feature requires founder, legal/provider, finance-provider, technical, security, and publication review before public launch.
