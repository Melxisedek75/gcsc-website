## Recommended First Viewport

### Badge

Use:

```text
Construction Trust Infrastructure
```

Do not use:

```text
Decentralized construction finance
Blockchain escrow
Tokenized contractor reputation
AI-managed DAO
```

### Headline

Use:

```text
Trust infrastructure for construction workflows
```

This is acceptable because it explains the product without implying live finance, live escrow, token value, or provider approval.

### Supporting Copy

Use:

```text
GCSC helps homeowners and contractors organize verified project records, milestone evidence, dispute packets, and partner-reviewed working-capital readiness. Web3 infrastructure remains a future regulated layer behind the workflow.
```

Why this works:

- "organize" avoids claiming regulated execution;
- "partner-reviewed" avoids claiming GCSC originates loans;
- "future regulated layer" keeps Web3 visible but not live;
- "behind the workflow" keeps blockchain out of the first public promise.

### Status Chips

Keep visible:

```text
Internal Draft - Not Approved For Publication
Publication Gate: NO-GO
Scope: No Real Money
```

These chips must be removed or changed only after a separate publication GO record.
