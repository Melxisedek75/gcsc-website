# GCSC Whitepaper v1.2 Founder Review Checklist

Status: founder review only. Do not edit `whitepaper.html` yet.

## Review Goal

Use this checklist to decide what should happen to the v1.2 whitepaper restructure draft before any published whitepaper file is changed.

The goal is to keep GCSC focused on a practical first launch:

- property owners and contractors meet inside SmartContractor;
- accepted bids become project contracts;
- milestones, evidence, disputes, audit trails, and escrow-ready payment records are tracked first in the database;
- contractor credit is described as a controlled workflow, not an automatic loan promise;
- token, DAO, stablecoin, Real Estate DAO, and smart-contract modules stay as later regulated infrastructure until attorney/provider/founder approval.

## Recommended Reading Order

1. Read `docs/whitepaper-v1-2-restructure-draft.md`.
2. Mark each decision below as approve, revise, or block.
3. Add short founder notes in the template at the bottom.
4. Only after approval, prepare a separate whitepaper edit plan.

## Decision 1: Project Structure

Choose one:

- approve 3-part structure;
- revise into 2-part structure;
- block restructure and keep current whitepaper layout.

Default recommendation: approve 3-part structure because it separates launch product, trust layer, and regulated settlement layer.

## Decision 2: First Product Positioning

Choose the Part 1 name:

- SmartContractor Marketplace;
- Construction Contract Marketplace;
- Smart Construction Operating Layer.

Default recommendation: SmartContractor Marketplace because it is simple for property owners, contractors, testers, partners, and non-crypto readers.

## Decision 3: Contractor Credit Placement

Choose one:

- keep contractor credit in Part 1 as a deposit-risk reduction workflow;
- move most contractor credit language into Part 2 as reputation and risk scoring;
- move contractor credit into Part 3 as later regulated finance.

Default recommendation: keep a conservative Part 1 explanation, but repeat all safety gates: no real lending, no real escrow, no real token collateral, no token price promise, and no irreversible approval before attorney/provider/founder approval.

## Decision 4: Token Economics Placement

Choose one:

- keep GCSC/GCST token economics early;
- move GCSC/GCST token economics after marketplace, reputation, AI, compliance, and legal boundaries;
- move token economics to an appendix for the next public draft.

Default recommendation: move GCSC/GCST token economics later so the whitepaper reads as construction infrastructure first and token utility second.

## Decision 5: Real Estate DAO Placement

Choose one:

- keep Real Estate DAO in the main whitepaper;
- move Real Estate DAO to a later expansion section;
- move Real Estate DAO to an appendix or separate follow-up document.

Default recommendation: move Real Estate DAO to later expansion so the first product does not look too broad before SmartContractor launch proof.

## Decision 6: CLARITY And Compliance Language

Choose one:

- mention the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act as an evolving policy reference only;
- remove named legislation and use general digital asset compliance wording;
- defer all regulatory framing to attorney review.

Default recommendation: mention the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act carefully as a policy reference, with no legal conclusion and no claim that it makes GCSC, GCST, escrow, lending, token collateral, or stablecoin settlement automatically legal.

Required safety wording:

- compliance-ready, not compliance-guaranteed;
- no legal conclusion;
- attorney/provider/founder approval required before real financial workflows;
- no real escrow;
- no real lending;
- no real token collateral;
- no token price promise.

## Decision 7: Publish Path

Choose one:

- approve this checklist and ask Codex to create a whitepaper edit plan;
- revise the checklist before planning;
- block publishing changes until legal/provider review.

Default recommendation: approve the structure first, then create an edit plan, then update the published whitepaper in a separate scoped change.

- edit `whitepaper.html` yet;
- publish a new PDF;
- claim legal status from pending legislation;
- describe automatic real escrow, real lending, real token collateral, or real stablecoin settlement;
