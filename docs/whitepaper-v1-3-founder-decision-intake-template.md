# GCSC Whitepaper v1.3 Founder Decision Intake Template

Status: internal founder decision intake template. No founder decision is recorded in this template yet.

This template does not approve public publication, public website replacement, legal conclusions, provider commitments, live Supabase changes, real payments, real loans, escrow custody, stablecoin settlement, token collateral, wallet signatures, FIO registrations, XPR signatures, or Metallicus/XPR partnership claims.

## Purpose

Use this template when the founder is ready to record a v1.3 direction decision. This separates internal direction approval from public publication approval and live action approval.

## Decision Meaning

| Decision | Meaning | Codex Allowed Next Step |
|---|---|---|
| HOLD | keep v1.3 internal | continue safe local prep only |
| REVISE | rewrite selected sections | prepare local revisions and validators |
| APPROVE_LOCAL_DIRECTION | use Construction Trust Infrastructure first as internal direction | continue local implementation and review prep |
| ROUTE_TO_REVIEWERS | founder may send packets to chosen reviewers | prepare packet refinements, but do not send |
| PREPARE_PUBLICATION_LATER | keep building publication package | keep publication gate NO-GO until all evidence is complete |

## Required Separate Approvals

The following require separate explicit approval and cannot be inferred from any founder direction decision:

- replacing `whitepaper.html`;
- replacing `index.html`;
- publishing PDF, deck, social, email, or website updates;
- contacting attorneys, providers, banks, insurers, appraisers, regulators, FIO, Metallicus, XPR, WebAuth, or Metal ecosystem contacts;
- live Supabase changes;
- real payments, real loans, escrow custody, stablecoin settlement, token collateral, wallet signatures, FIO registrations, minting, staking, bridging, swaps, or transfers;
- legal, securities, lending, escrow, tax, insurance, appraisal, or contractor-licensing conclusions.

## Safe Intake Rules

- record only the founder decision and scope;
- do not paste secrets;
- do not paste account credentials;
- do not convert internal direction approval into publication approval;
- do not convert reviewer routing approval into autonomous outreach;
- do not convert public wording approval into live finance or Web3 approval.

## Stop Boundary

If the founder wants public publication, live integrations, external outreach, legal conclusions, payments, loans, escrow, stablecoin settlement, token collateral, FIO registrations, XPR signatures, or account changes, create a separate approval packet and stop until the exact approval is recorded.
