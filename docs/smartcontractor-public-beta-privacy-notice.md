# SmartContractor Public Beta Privacy Notice

## Purpose

This draft gives public beta testers a simple privacy notice before they test SmartContractor. It is designed for demo only testing and helps prevent testers from submitting private data, secrets, payment data, wallet data, or real customer information.

Use this with `PUBLIC_SITE_URL`, the tester FAQ, consent acknowledgement, launch message, known issues, and support queue.

## Plain English Notice

SmartContractor public beta is an early demo test. The founder is collecting feedback about product clarity, page flow, Magic Link login clarity if enabled, mobile/PWA usability, issue reports, and safe request tracing.

The beta is not ready for production private data. Testers should use demo information only.

During public beta:

- real payments disabled;
- real loans disabled;
- escrow disabled;
- token collateral disabled;
- no production customer onboarding;
- no production document collection.

## What Testers Should Not Submit

Testers must not submit:

- no SQL;
- no secrets;
- passwords;
- private keys or seed phrases;
- Supabase tokens;
- database URLs;
- payment data;
- bank data;
- card data;
- wallet data;
- personal IDs;
- private contact details;
- real customer addresses;
- real contractor license documents;
- real insurance documents;
- unredacted screenshots or recordings.

## What Safe Feedback Can Include

Safe feedback can include:

- tester role;
- `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` tested;
- page or flow name;
- expected result;
- actual result;
- device and browser;
- safe `X-Request-Id` if visible;
- issue severity estimate;
- redacted screenshot if useful.

## Evidence Handling

Screenshots, recordings, and logs should be treated as local founder review evidence first.

Before sharing outside founder/admin review:

1. Remove private contact details.
2. Remove addresses, account data, payment data, wallet data, and IDs.
3. Remove database URLs, tokens, and secret-looking values.
4. Keep only redacted screenshots or summaries.
5. Link evidence to an issue ID or decision log entry.

Raw unredacted evidence should not be sent to partners, public channels, grants, investors, or providers.

## Founder Review Required

Founder review is required before:

- any screenshot or tester quote leaves local review;
- any public claim is made from tester feedback;
- any legal review, provider review, payment, loan, escrow, or token collateral decision is implied;
- any privacy policy, terms of service, or production data collection language is presented as final.

## Not A Full Privacy Policy

This is not a full privacy policy and not legal advice. It is a beta safety notice.

Before public production launch, the founder must complete legal review and approve a formal privacy policy, terms of service, data retention rules, deletion process, payment provider terms, and user-support process.
