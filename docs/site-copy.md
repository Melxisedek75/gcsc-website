## FAQ (10 questions)

**Q: What is SmartContractor?**
A: A mobile app that connects homeowners with verified construction contractors. Every job is a milestone contract — money releases only when you approve verifiable work. Built on XPR Network so every payment is on-chain.

**Q: Do I need a crypto wallet?**
A: Yes — WebAuth wallet, free, biometric, sub-two-minute setup. We don't accept credit cards because credit cards don't give you milestone-by-milestone control over the money.

**Q: What does it cost?**
A: Homeowners pay a 25 XPR verification fee per posted job (covers AI compliance and dispute coverage). No subscriptions, no platform commission on the work itself. Contractors pay 50 XPR per verified lead with a replace guarantee on the first purchase.

**Q: What if the contractor doesn't finish?**
A: You only ever release what's been approved. The remaining escrow stays under your control. If you escalate, AI compliance reviews the milestone proof and the dispute layer kicks in.

**Q: What if I don't approve a finished milestone?**
A: The contractor can request mediation. AI compliance reviews the proof against the agreed scope. If the work is verifiable but you refuse, mediation can release on the contractor's behalf — but they need proof, not just an invoice.

**Q: Where do my funds sit?**
A: In a smart contract on XPR Network. Not in our bank account. We can't touch your money — only you can release it, and only to milestones you've approved.

**Q: Is XPR Network safe?**
A: XPR Network has been live since 2020, secured by a federation of block producers, and audited by third parties. SmartContractor's contracts will be audited by CertiK and Hacken before mainnet launch.

**Q: How do you verify contractors?**
A: License lookup against state databases, insurance verification, on-chain reputation from completed jobs, and homeowner ratings. AI compliance re-checks every quarter.

**Q: Can I use this for commercial construction?**
A: SmartContractor is built for residential and small-commercial work right now. Larger commercial projects (multi-unit, industrial) are on the roadmap.

**Q: When does mainnet launch?**
A: We're in beta on XPR testnet through 2026. Mainnet launch follows audit completion and a pilot in Seattle. Sign up for the waitlist for the launch date.

## Tone notes

- Lead with what we do, not how. "Hire the contractor. Pay only for the work that's done." beats "Decentralized construction platform powered by blockchain."
- Never promise prices, never quote APYs, never describe GCSC token as an investment.
- "On-chain proof" and "milestone escrow" are the load-bearing phrases — repeat them.
- Avoid: revolutionary, disrupting, game-changer, paradigm shift, Web3 jargon, "the future of X".
- US English. Sentence case for headings (not title case).
