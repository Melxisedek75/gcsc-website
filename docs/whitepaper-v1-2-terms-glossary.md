# GCSC Whitepaper v1.2 Terms Glossary

Status: internal wording guard. Do not publish this file as a legal glossary.

Purpose: keep v1.2 whitepaper language consistent before any public edit, PDF update, partner packet, grant packet, investor packet, deck, email, social excerpt, or announcement is used.

## Preferred Terms

Use these terms as the safe default:

- SmartContractor Marketplace
- project contracts
- milestones
- Contractor Reputation Layer
- AI-assisted review
- AI boundaries
- escrow-ready payment workflow
- credit-ready contractor workflow
- stablecoin settlement roadmap
- tokenized construction agreements
- Digital Asset Market Clarity Act as policy context

## Terms Requiring Review

These terms may be useful, but they require attorney/provider/founder approval before public use:

- regulated construction-financial infrastructure network
- escrow workflow
- contractor credit
- working-capital credit
- stablecoin settlement engine
- tokenized construction agreement
- on-chain construction finance
- policy context under the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act

When used, the text must say this is not a legal conclusion.

## Blocked Terms

Do not use these claims in public v1.2 language:

- real escrow is live
- real lending is live
- real token collateral is live
- no real escrow review needed
- no real lending review needed
- no real token collateral review needed
- no token price promise
- no guaranteed yield
- no automatic AI legal or financial decision
- guaranteed approval
- guaranteed contractor credit
- guaranteed investment return

## Replacement Rules

- Replace "escrow" with "escrow-ready workflow" unless founder/legal/provider review approves stronger wording.
- Replace "loans are available" with "credit-ready contractor workflow is planned and gated".
- Replace "token collateral" with "token collateral roadmap" unless legal/provider/security review approves activation language.
- Replace "AI decides" with "AI-assisted review with human/founder/provider approval gates".
- Replace any token-price language with utility, governance, settlement roadmap, or no token price promise language.

## Safe Default

If wording is uncertain, use conservative language: SmartContractor Marketplace first, project contracts and milestones second, Contractor Reputation Layer third, then stablecoin settlement roadmap and tokenized construction agreements only as future regulated roadmap items. Keep no real escrow, no real lending, no real token collateral, no token price promise, no guaranteed yield, and no automatic AI legal or financial decision boundaries visible until attorney/provider/founder approval is recorded.
