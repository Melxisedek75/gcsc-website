# GCSC Whitepaper v1.3 SmartContractor Wording Alignment

Status: internal wording alignment. This does not publish public changes.

## Purpose

SmartContractor must support the v1.3 direction: Construction Trust Infrastructure first, future regulated Web3 construction infrastructure second.

This document does not approve live finance, escrow custody, token collateral, stablecoin settlement, FIO actions, XPR signatures, Metallicus partnership claims, or public publication. Current SmartContractor product wording scan rows are tracked in `docs/whitepaper-v1-3-smartcontractor-wording-evidence-log.md` without editing product files.

## Safe Product Position

SmartContractor should speak as construction workflow software:

- project records;
- contractor profiles;
- bids;
- milestones;
- evidence;
- dispute packets;
- reputation;
- audit logs;
- request IDs;
- partner-reviewed working-capital readiness;
- escrow-ready milestone records.

## Blocked Product Claims

SmartContractor product-facing copy must not claim:

- investment product;
- yield or passive income;
- instant loan approval;
- live escrow custody by GCSC;
- automatic on-chain payment release;
- public NFT marketplace;
- token collateral as live;
- stablecoin settlement as live;
- approved Metallicus/FIO/XPR partnership;
- legal, lending, escrow, money-transmission, insurance, appraisal, or contractor-license approval.

## Allowed Context For Risky Terms

Risky terms may appear only when the sentence is clearly a boundary, risk register, blocked action, research-only note, or future-review note.

Examples of allowed context:

- "Demo-only loan requests create local review records only."
- "They do not approve credit, fund a contractor, route repayment, release escrow, or lock token collateral."
- "Research-only. No partnership, endorsement, integration, account setup, or provider approval is claimed."
- "Future provider-reviewed settlement path."

## Product Copy Rule

Every user-facing finance/Web3 statement must answer four questions:

1. Is this live today? If not, say it is demo-only, future, or review-required.
2. Who performs the regulated function? If regulated, say licensed/provider-reviewed partner or future licensed entity.
3. What does GCSC do now? Say records, workflow, evidence, readiness, or review packet.
4. What is blocked? Say no real money movement, no approval, no custody, no partnership claim, and no legal conclusion where relevant.
