# GCSC Whitepaper v1.2 Founder Approval Brief

Status: internal founder approval brief. Do not treat this file as approval.

Purpose: give the founder one short review surface before any public v1.2 whitepaper edit, excerpt, PDF, website copy, deck, partner packet, grant packet, investor packet, email, social post, or announcement is used.

## Founder Decision Needed

The founder should approve or reject these choices:

- Use SmartContractor Marketplace as the first public product narrative.
- Present project contracts and milestones as the first operating layer.
- Present Contractor Reputation Layer as the trust layer.
- Present AI-assisted workflows with visible AI boundaries.
- Present escrow-ready and credit-ready infrastructure as gated roadmap language.
- Present stablecoin settlement roadmap and tokenized construction agreements only as future regulated roadmap items.
- Treat Digital Asset Market Clarity Act references as policy context, not a legal conclusion.
- Keep Real Estate DAO, token economics, and GCSC/GCST utility after the marketplace and trust narrative.

## Must Stay Blocked

Public v1.2 language must not claim:

- real escrow is live;
- real lending is live;
- real token collateral is live;
- token price promise;
- guaranteed yield;
- guaranteed contractor credit;
- AI makes automatic legal or financial decisions;
- attorney/provider/founder approval is optional.
