# Final Submission Instructions

Use this as the simple, step-by-step path for the person uploading the bid.

## Before Upload Day

1. Open OpenGov Procurement: https://procurement.opengov.com/
2. Sign in to the company account.
3. Find solicitation 2026-SWR-031.
4. Download every file and addendum again.
5. Compare downloaded files to the bid package we prepared.
6. Fill the official bid form from Pierce County.
7. Fill all required certifications from the official packet.
8. Get the bid bond from surety/bank if required.
9. Upload background check forms if required at bid time.
10. Confirm final price and signer authority.

## Upload Package Naming

Use clean names:

- `KNB_Bid_Form_2026-SWR-031.pdf`
- `KNB_Bid_Bond_2026-SWR-031.pdf`
- `KNB_ACM_Compliance_2026-SWR-031.pdf`
- `KNB_Background_Checks_2026-SWR-031.pdf`
- `KNB_Technical_Bid_Package_2026-SWR-031.pdf`
- `KNB_Addenda_Acknowledgement_2026-SWR-031.pdf`

## Submission Day: May 20, 2026

Submit before 12:00 PM if possible. Deadline is 1:00 PM.

1. Log in.
2. Open solicitation 2026-SWR-031.
3. Check whether any new addendum was issued.
4. Complete every required online field.
5. Upload required documents.
6. Review every upload.
7. Confirm final bid amount matches the signed bid form.
8. Submit.
9. Save confirmation page/screenshot.
10. Save confirmation email.

## Do Not Do

- Do not submit without checking addenda.
- Do not use our draft forms if Pierce County requires official forms.
- Do not leave price blank.
- Do not miss bid bond if required.
- Do not wait until the last 30 minutes.
- Do not include unsupported claims like "asbestos free" beyond what the survey actually tested.

## After Submission

- Keep phone/email available for clarification.
- Save all vendor quotes.
- Prepare submittals immediately in case of award.
- Prepare Intent to Pay Prevailing Wage if awarded.
- Keep background-approved crew ready.
