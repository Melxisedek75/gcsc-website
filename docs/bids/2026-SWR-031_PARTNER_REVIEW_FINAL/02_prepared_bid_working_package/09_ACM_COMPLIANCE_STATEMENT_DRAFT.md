## Statement

In accordance with applicable Washington asbestos communication and public works requirements, the undersigned contractor acknowledges receipt and review of the Limited Hazardous Materials Survey Report prepared for the CCB First Floor Door Widening project.

The contractor understands that the report identifies the tested materials and survey findings for the project area. Based on the reviewed report summary, asbestos was not detected in the tested samples and lead was not detected in the tested paint samples. The contractor will communicate the report information to workers and subcontractors before disturbance of affected materials.

If suspect materials are encountered that are not identified in the report, or if concealed conditions differ from the issued documents, the contractor will stop work in the affected area, protect the area, notify Pierce County, and await direction before disturbing the material.

The contractor further agrees to comply with owner procedures, applicable regulations, and safe work practices throughout demolition and construction.
