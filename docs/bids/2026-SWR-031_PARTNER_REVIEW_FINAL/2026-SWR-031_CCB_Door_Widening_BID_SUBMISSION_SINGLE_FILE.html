The base bid includes labor, materials, equipment, supervision, coordination, and closeout necessary to complete the first-floor door widening work shown in the bid documents.

Quality Control Plan

Purpose

This plan describes how the contractor will control quality for a public-building interior door widening project involving fire-rated glazing, hollow metal frames, cold-formed metal framing, gypsum assemblies, existing finish restoration, stainless trim, sealants, and ceiling repair.

Trade leadsWorkmanship, product compliance, installation instructions, inspections.

Inspection Hold Points

  • Before demolition: photo-document existing conditions and protection.
  • After selective demolition: verify concealed conditions, dimensions, substrate, and conflicts.
  • Before frame/glazing order release: verify rough opening dimensions.
  • Before closing walls: verify framing, anchorage, backing, fire/acoustic perimeter conditions.
  • Before finish install: verify GWB finish level, substrate flatness, and transitions.
  • Before final inspection: verify glazing labels, frame labels, sealants, stainless finish, ceiling match, cleanup.

Product Compliance

Fire-rated glazing:

  • Use specified basis-of-design product or approved equal only.
  • Confirm rating, safety glazing requirements, thickness, labeling, and compatible frame system.
  • Keep manufacturer data and installation instructions in closeout file.

Hollow metal frames:

  • Confirm gauge, rating, anchors, labels, and prep.
  • Protect primer/finish until final acceptance.

Gypsum / CBB / finishes:

  • Use Type X gypsum where specified.
  • Finish visible GWB surfaces to specified level.
  • Use compatible backing for granite/terrazzo/tile areas.

Sealants:

  • Use specified or approved sealants.
  • Confirm joint prep, primer need, backing, tooling, and color match.

Existing finishes:

  • Salvage and reinstall only pieces approved for reuse.
  • Protect edges and surfaces from chips, stains, and scratches.

Closeout Records

  • Product data and approved submittals.
  • Fire-rated glazing/frame documentation.
  • Daily photo log.
  • Inspection/punch list records.
  • Final cleaning confirmation.
  • Warranty information if required.

Site Safety And Logistics Plan

Project Context

The work is inside the County-City Building at 930 Tacoma Ave S, Tacoma, WA. The building is an active public facility. Safety planning must protect workers, county staff, visitors, existing finishes, and public circulation.

Site Access

  • All workers must complete required background checks before entering controlled work areas.
  • Workers must follow Pierce County sign-in, badge, escort, and access rules.
  • Deliveries must be coordinated with building operations.
  • Parking/loading limitations in downtown Tacoma must be confirmed during the pre-bid walkthrough.

Public Protection

  • Install temporary barriers and signage approved by the owner.
  • Maintain clean and code-compliant paths of travel.
  • Keep tools, cords, materials, and debris inside the controlled work area.
  • Protect floors, walls, granite, terrazzo, stainless, ceiling, and lobby finishes.
  • Maintain daily cleanup before leaving site.

Dust, Noise, And Indoor Air

  • Use dust control during demolition, cutting, sanding, and finishing.
  • Use HEPA vacuuming or equivalent cleanup where appropriate.
  • Coordinate noisy work with building operations.
  • Keep waste containers covered and remove debris promptly.

Hazardous Materials

The issued hazardous materials survey reports no asbestos detected in tested samples and no lead detected in tested paint samples. If suspicious material is encountered that is not covered by the report:

1. Stop work in the affected area. 2. Protect the area. 3. Notify Pierce County immediately. 4. Do not disturb the material until the owner gives direction.

Worker Safety Controls

  • PPE: safety glasses, gloves, hearing protection, dust protection as task requires.
  • Ladders/scaffolds: inspect before use and follow manufacturer requirements.
  • Cutting/drilling: control sparks, dust, chips, and noise.
  • Manual handling: use team lifts for heavy glass, frames, stone, and stainless.
  • Fire/life safety: maintain extinguishers where hot work or spark-producing work is allowed.

Daily Closeout

  • Remove debris.
  • Vacuum/sweep affected areas.
  • Confirm barriers are secure.
  • Protect unfinished work.
  • Log work completed, inspections, issues, and next-day plan.

Prevailing Wage And Compliance Plan

Purpose

This project appears to be a Washington public works project for Pierce County. The contractor must comply with applicable prevailing wage, certified payroll, responsible bidder, safety, and owner documentation requirements.

Official Rules To Verify Before Submission

  • Pierce County prevailing wage monitoring: https://www.piercecountywa.gov/2253/Prevailing-Wage-Monitoring
  • Washington L&I prevailing wage lookup: https://www.lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/public-works-projects/prevailing-wage-rates/index
  • Washington L&I worker guidance: https://www.lni.wa.gov/workers-rights/wages/prevailing-wage-rates

Washington L&I guidance states that wage classification depends on the actual work performed, county, and the effective date. L&I also states that the effective date is generally the prime contractor bid due date, or contract award date if award is more than six months after bid due date.

For this bid, use Pierce County and the bid due date of May 20, 2026 unless the bid packet/addendum instructs otherwise.
