## What This Package Is

This package is for partner review before deciding and finalizing the bid. It includes the original owner documents plus a prepared working bid package with scope, checklist, pricing worksheet, schedule, safety/logistics plan, quality control plan, prevailing wage plan, RFQ text, and walkthrough questions.

## What Partners Should Review

1. Can we realistically perform this work within 42 calendar days?
2. Who will cover each trade scope: demolition, framing/drywall, fire-rated glazing, hollow metal frames, granite/terrazzo, stainless, ceiling, sealants?
3. Do we have access to required Washington prevailing wage labor?
4. Who can attend the May 13, 2026 pre-bid walkthrough at 7:00 AM?
5. What vendor quotes are needed immediately, especially fire-rated glass and hollow metal frames?
6. What is our final bid number after quotes, labor, bond, tax treatment, and risk allowance?
7. Who is the authorized signer and who will upload the bid through OpenGov?

The prepared documents are professional working drafts. For final submission, use official Pierce County/OpenGov forms wherever required. Do not replace official bid forms, bonds, certifications, or addenda acknowledgments with drafts unless the portal allows supporting attachments.
