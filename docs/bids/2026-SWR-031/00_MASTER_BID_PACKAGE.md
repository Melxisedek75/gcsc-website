## Executive Bid Strategy

This is a small but sensitive public building interior project. The winning bid should communicate three things clearly:

- We understand the building is active and public-facing.
- We understand the critical scope: widening/opening work, fire-rated glazing, hollow metal frames, Type X GWB, Level 5 finish, salvage/reinstall of existing granite/terrazzo elements, stainless trim, ceiling repair, and clean closeout.
- We will comply with Washington public works rules, background checks, site security, certified payroll, and prevailing wage requirements.

## Tacoma / Pierce County Factors To Price

Use a Tacoma-specific risk allowance. This is not a generic residential remodel:

- Public building access: work may be constrained by business hours, security, escorts, background checks, and restricted staging.
- Prevailing wage labor: labor cost should be priced using Washington State L&I rates locked to the bid due date / applicable effective date.
- Specialty procurement: 90-minute fire-rated Pilkington Pyrostop or approved equal can carry lead time and freight risk.
- Existing finish protection: granite, terrazzo, stainless, lobby flooring, ceiling grid, public path, and adjacent surfaces need daily protection.
- Inspection / coordination: architect, owner, fire/life-safety, and closeout documentation need time in the schedule.
- Parking and delivery: downtown Tacoma delivery windows and loading/staging limits should be confirmed during the pre-bid walkthrough.
