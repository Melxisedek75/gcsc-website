# Scope And Price Proposal

Project: CCB First Floor Door Widening  
Location: County-City Building, Tacoma, WA  
Bid due: May 20, 2026, 1:00 PM

## Base Bid Scope

The base bid includes labor, materials, equipment, supervision, coordination, and closeout necessary to complete the first-floor door widening work shown in the bid documents.

Included work:

- Project mobilization, site protection, and daily cleanup.
- Coordination with Pierce County security, building operations, architect, and inspectors.
- Selective demolition in the first-floor lobby/work area.
- Salvage, protection, storage, and reinstallation/coordination of designated existing granite/terrazzo finishes where required by drawings/specifications.
- Removal/rework of existing GWB, stainless trim, ceiling components, and related finishes in the affected work area.
- Cold-formed metal framing and required anchorage.
- Type X gypsum board, cementitious backer board where required, taping, finishing, and paint-ready Level 5 surfaces where specified.
- Hollow metal frames meeting specified fire-rating and SDI requirements.
- Fire-rated glazing system, including 90-minute-rated glass matching specified basis of design or approved equivalent.
- Stainless steel trim/protection at modified openings as required.
- Sealants, acoustic sealant, perimeter sealing, and finish transitions.
- Acoustic ceiling repair/reinstallation to match existing conditions.
- Final cleaning, punch list, and closeout documents.

## Exclusions / Clarifications

These items should be clearly confirmed before bid submission:

- Work outside the documented first-floor lobby/door widening limits.
- Hazardous material abatement beyond the issued survey. The survey indicates no asbestos detected in tested samples and no lead detected in tested paint samples; unknown materials discovered later should be handled under owner direction.
- Structural steel or concealed structural repairs not shown in the bid documents.
- After-hours work unless required by owner/security constraints or later addendum.
- Utility relocation beyond items specifically shown in drawings.
- Permit fees unless expressly assigned to contractor in the bid packet.
- Sales tax treatment to follow bid instructions and Pierce County requirements.

## Recommended Bid Position

Do not bid at the published budget simply because it is listed. Price from quotes and labor plan. The largest risk items are fire-rated glazing, hollow metal frames, downtown Tacoma logistics, prevailing wage labor, and preserving/reinstalling existing finishes.

Before final submission, obtain hard quotes for:

- Pilkington Pyrostop or approved fire-rated glazing system.
- Hollow metal frames and anchors/fire-rating labels.
- Stainless steel trim.
- Granite/terrazzo handling.
- Any after-hours premium requested by building operations.
