## Fire-Rated Glass / Glazing RFQ

Request quote for:

- 90-minute fire-rated glazing per bid documents.
- Basis of design: Pilkington Pyrostop or approved equal if allowed.
- Required thickness, labels, safety glazing compliance, installation accessories, and compatible system.
- Field measurement requirements.
- Freight and lead time to Tacoma, WA.
- Installer labor if included.

Questions for vendor:

- Is the specified product available in required size by project schedule?
- What frame system compatibility is required?
- What are exact lead times after approved submittals?
- Are there substitutions that meet rating and code, and do they require formal substitution request by May 15, 2026?
