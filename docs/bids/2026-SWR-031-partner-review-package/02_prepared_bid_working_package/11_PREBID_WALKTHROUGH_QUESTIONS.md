# Pre-Bid Walkthrough Questions

Walkthrough: May 13, 2026 at 7:00 AM  
Location: first floor entrance from Tacoma Ave  
Goal: verify field conditions, logistics, schedule constraints, hidden risks, and owner expectations before final pricing.

## Bring To Walkthrough

- Printed drawings.
- Printed specs / key pages.
- Tape measure / laser measure.
- Phone/camera.
- Notebook.
- PPE.
- List of questions below.

## Field Verification

1. Confirm exact work limits and all surfaces to protect.
2. Confirm dimensions of existing opening and proposed opening.
3. Confirm ceiling height, grid type, tile match, and support conditions.
4. Confirm existing wall construction and any concealed utilities.
5. Confirm HVAC sensor/item shown for removal or relocation and who is responsible.
6. Confirm which granite/terrazzo pieces must be salvaged and reinstalled.
7. Confirm stainless trim profile and finish expectations.
8. Confirm whether after-hours work is required or optional.
9. Confirm whether noisy demolition/cutting work must happen outside public hours.
10. Confirm inspection hold points and who schedules them.

## Logistics

1. Where can contractor park?
2. Where can deliveries unload?
3. Is there an elevator/loading route?
4. Where can materials be staged?
5. Where can debris be removed?
6. What hours can contractor access the site?
7. Are escorts required?
8. How long do background checks take?
9. Is a temporary barrier plan required before mobilization?
10. Are there restrictions on adhesives, sealants, odors, dust, or noise?

## Pricing / Contract Questions

1. Is sales tax included in the bid form or added separately?
2. What bid bond percentage and format are required?
3. Are permit fees by owner or contractor?
4. Are substitutions allowed for fire-rated glass or must the basis-of-design be used?
5. Are there known long-lead concerns with specified glazing?
6. Are any addenda expected before May 15?
7. Is the project funded only by local/state funds or are federal wage rules involved?
8. Which wage classifications does Pierce County expect for the main scopes?
9. Are certified payroll submissions required weekly through a specific system?
10. What closeout documents are mandatory for final payment?

## Photo Log Checklist

- Existing door/opening conditions.
- Granite/terrazzo panels and edges.
- Stainless trim.
- Ceiling grid and tile.
- Floor protection route.
- Loading/delivery route.
- Adjacent public circulation.
- Any concealed/visible utilities near work.
