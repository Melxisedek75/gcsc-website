## Review Meaning

- Accept means keep an internal candidate alive for later review.
- Revise means update internal wording and rerun checks.
- Reject means remove the candidate from future public wording.
- Hold means route to legal/provider, finance-provider, or technical review.

None of these states approves live loans, real escrow, token collateral, stablecoin settlement, repayment routing, public lending claims, guaranteed funding, instant approval, AI loan approval, AI automatic payment release, or GCSC acting as a lender, bank, broker, licensed finance provider, or escrow agent.
