# GCSC Whitepaper v1.2 Internal Redline Preview

Status: internal redline preview. This is not a published whitepaper edit and does not replace `whitepaper.html`.

Purpose: turn the founder-approved v1.2 direction into a safe preview of what would change in the public whitepaper after approvals are recorded.

## Redline Scope

The preview should prepare future edits only for these approved internal themes:

- SmartContractor Marketplace as the first public product narrative.
- Project contracts and milestones as the first operating layer.
- Contractor Reputation Layer as the trust and quality layer.
- AI-assisted workflows with explicit AI boundaries.
- Escrow-ready and credit-ready infrastructure as gated roadmap language.
- Stablecoin settlement roadmap and tokenized construction agreements as future regulated roadmap items.
- Digital Asset Market Clarity Act as policy context, not a legal conclusion.
- Real Estate DAO, token economics, and GCSC/GCST utility after the marketplace and trust narrative.

## Section Preview

Future public redline should follow this order:

1. Opening: position GCSC as a construction operating network led by SmartContractor Marketplace.
2. Product layer: explain homeowner, contractor, project contract, milestone, evidence, dispute, and peer review flow.
3. Trust layer: explain Contractor Reputation Layer, compliance signals, ratings, dispute history, and AI-assisted routing.
4. Finance layer: describe escrow-ready payments, credit-ready contractor workflows, and disabled real-money gates.
5. Settlement roadmap: describe GCST and stablecoin settlement only as a reviewed future roadmap, not a live payment promise.
6. Tokenized agreement roadmap: describe tokenized construction agreements as future regulated infrastructure, not current securities, loans, or collateral.
7. Governance and assets: place GCSC/GCST utility, token economics, DAO governance, and Real Estate DAO after product readiness.
8. Risk gates: keep founder, attorney, provider, technical, strict RLS/admin, and security review gates visible.

## Must Not Enter Public Redline

The redline preview must not introduce:

- real escrow is live;
- real lending is live;
- real token collateral is live;
- guaranteed contractor credit;
- token price promise;
- guaranteed yield;
- AI makes automatic legal or financial decisions;
- Digital Asset Market Clarity Act creates legal approval for GCSC;
- attorney/provider/founder approval is optional.
