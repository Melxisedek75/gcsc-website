# GCSC Whitepaper v1.2 Founder Review Worksheet

Status: internal founder review worksheet. This is not approval to publish or edit `whitepaper.html`.

Purpose: give the founder a simple accept/revise/reject worksheet for the v1.2 whitepaper direction before any public whitepaper, PDF, website, deck, partner packet, grant packet, investor packet, email, social post, or announcement language is changed.

## How To Use

Review each item and mark one of:

- Accept: safe to keep in the future public edit after required approvals.
- Revise: concept is useful, but wording or placement needs changes.
- Reject: do not use in the future public edit.

Do not treat any unchecked item as approved.

## Must Stay Blocked

Reject any wording that says:

- real escrow is live;
- real lending is live;
- real token collateral is live;
- contractor credit is guaranteed;
- token price is promised;
- yield is guaranteed;
- AI makes automatic legal or financial decisions;
- Digital Asset Market Clarity Act creates legal approval for GCSC;
- attorney/provider/founder approval is optional.

## Approval Record Needed

Before publication, the approval record must show:

- founder approval recorded;
- attorney/provider/founder approval recorded for escrow, lending, token collateral, stablecoin settlement, and tokenized construction agreement wording;
- technical approval recorded for MVP readiness, strict RLS/admin readiness, and disabled real-money gates;
- verification commands completed.
