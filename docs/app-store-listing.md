## Full description (Play Store / App Store — under 4000 chars)

SmartContractor is the trust layer between homeowners and construction professionals. Every job is structured as a milestone contract: contractors get paid only after you approve verifiable work, never upfront.

**For homeowners**
- Post a job in under two minutes. Verified contractors respond with bids.
- Funds sit in escrow on XPR Network — released milestone by milestone after your sign-off.
- Every approval and every payment is recorded on-chain. You can inspect the transaction any time.
- AI compliance checks license, insurance, and dispute history before a bid lands in your inbox.

**For contractors**
- Pay 50 XPR per lead, get a verified homeowner job. Replace guarantee on your first purchase.
- Submit bids with timeline, materials, and message. Win, get paid as you complete milestones.
- No middlemen taking 20% off the top. No invoice chasing. No payment delays.

**How payments work**
1. Homeowner posts a job and funds escrow (25 XPR verification fee).
2. Contractor submits a bid. On selection, milestone payments are committed to escrow.
3. Contractor uploads proof at each milestone. AI compliance verifies. Homeowner approves.
4. Funds release instantly via WebAuth biometric tap. Sub-second confirmation, no platform fees.

**Built on XPR Network**
SmartContractor runs on XPR Network — a fast, fee-less blockchain designed for real-world payments. WebAuth wallet ships with biometric security and NFC support. No crypto knowledge required — onboarding takes two minutes.

**Privacy & security**
- Biometric authentication on every payment approval.
- No card numbers stored — payments go straight from your XPR wallet.
- License/insurance data verified by AI agents, never shared without consent.

Start with one job. Pay only when the work is done. That's the contract.
