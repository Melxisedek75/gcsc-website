# GCSC Vision Addendum — Marketplace, AI and Metal Ecosystem

Date: 2026-10-02
Status: INTERNAL VISION / FOUNDER REVIEW

This document records the product direction recovered from the current project context and today's founder request. It is an internal source for planning, grant preparation and future whitepaper revision. It is not a public launch claim, legal conclusion, grant award, or approval for live payments, lending, escrow or blockchain execution.

## Product thesis

GCSC is building construction-financial trust infrastructure. SmartContractor is the product layer where property owners, contractors and future institutional partners coordinate construction work through structured project records, bids, proposals, milestones, evidence, reputation and review.

## Marketplace direction

The platform should support two complementary market mechanisms:

1. Direct lead marketplace — an owner request is matched to relevant contractors or construction-service providers using explicit scope, geography, availability, compliance facts and fit signals.
2. Proposal/lead auction — where appropriate, qualified contractors may compete for access to a verified opportunity under transparent rules, bounded pricing and anti-spam controls. The platform must distinguish a lead-access auction from a financial investment product or token sale.

The first implementation remains a database-backed, demo-safe workflow. Any paid lead, auction settlement, token payment, refund, escrow or revenue distribution requires separate provider, legal, security and founder approval.

## Two-sided AI agent system

### Property-owner agent

- helps describe the repair or construction need;
- asks only the questions needed to make a useful request;
- explains scope, evidence, timing and budget assumptions in plain language;
- proposes the next step of saving a draft request and requesting proposals;
- compares proposals against owner priorities without making a legal, insurance or financing decision;
- keeps the owner in control before submission, sharing, payment or contract acceptance.

### Contractor/agency agent

- interprets the owner scope and identifies missing information;
- helps prepare a transparent proposal with inclusions, exclusions, assumptions, milestones and evidence;
- identifies license, insurance, schedule, material and site questions;
- helps an agency or contractor decide whether a lead is a good fit;
- can prepare a draft response, but cannot invent credentials, guarantee a win, approve a loan or bind a contract without human confirmation.

### Shared trust layer

Both agents use the same project facts, but receive role-appropriate views. They must preserve provenance, uncertainty, user confirmation, audit events and escalation to human/admin review. AI is an assistant and routing layer, not the final authority for legal conclusions, real-money movement, escrow release, credit approval or dispute adjudication.

## Metal / Metallicus integration direction

The project should approach Metallicus as a potential ecosystem, sandbox and infrastructure partner, not as an assumed grantor. The initial request should focus on:

- a controlled sandbox or technical discovery session;
- guidance on Metal/XPR/WebAuth-compatible identity and wallet flows;
- a construction marketplace use case for verified project records and future settlement;
- AI-assisted owner/contractor workflows with explicit compliance boundaries;
- a staged pilot plan with measurable local/demo milestones before any production financial integration.

The grant request should ask which current program, fund or partner pathway is appropriate rather than assert that a grant is available.

## Acceptance criteria

- owner can create a draft request without accidental submission;
- contractor can create a draft proposal with explicit assumptions;
- marketplace simulation shows matching and auction states without real payment;
- both AI roles preserve safety boundaries and do not fabricate credentials;
- every public claim links to current evidence;
- no live financial or blockchain action is enabled by this vision document.
